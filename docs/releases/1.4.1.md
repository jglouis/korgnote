Fixes for sync, and for list items written over several lines. Update the server too: its image now keeps the app's connection alive.

### Sync

- **Two notes are no longer merged into one file.** When two devices created a note with the same name, or one created a note under the name of another deleted or renamed away while it was edited elsewhere, sync could keep both in one file, with two IDs and two titles. The server's note now keeps the name, and the other is kept beside it as a conflict copy. A note already merged this way stays as it is: split it by hand.
- **Changes made elsewhere arrive again after a connection dies silently,** as when the phone sleeps or changes network. Notes could stay stale under "Last synced" with no warning; the app now notices within a minute and a half and catches up on what it missed. This needs the 1.4.1 server; with an older one, the app waits as before.
- **The app syncs when you come back to it,** which is when changes made on another device are most likely waiting.
- When comparing two versions of a note, each change is labelled with the heading it is under, not the one above it.

### Notes

- **A list item or footnote written over several lines keeps all of them.** The lines after the first showed as a separate paragraph; the reading view now joins them to the item and wraps them to the screen. A line ending in `\\` keeps its break, as in Org.
