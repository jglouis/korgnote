Capture: put a link, a place, some text or a calendar event into your notes from wherever it is, in one step. And a few fixes to how TODO keywords are read.

### Capture

- **Share to KorgNote from any app.** A page from the browser, a place from Google Maps, text you selected: choose KorgNote in the share sheet, or *Capture in KorgNote* in the text selection menu on Android.
- **Capture an event from your calendar,** Google Calendar's included, which can't share its events: *Capture an event* in the drawer, or the *Event* shortcut on the app's icon on Android. It is written as a `SCHEDULED:` entry, so it shows in your agenda.
- **Choose where it goes:** the inbox, another note, a new note, or a heading — to add to it, or to put a new entry under it. A place becomes the heading's `:LOCATION:`, an event its `SCHEDULED:`.
- **See the entry as it will be written,** shown and edited like any note, and mark it with one of the note's TODO keywords if you like. A place gives its location, its link, or both.
- Captures are filed even with the app closed on Android. On iPhone they are filed the next time you open KorgNote.
- Long-press the app's icon on Android for a quick note.
- `korgnote://capture?url=…&title=…&body=…` links capture too, the same as Emacs's org-protocol, for bookmarklets and Shortcuts.

### Notes

- **A heading starting with a word in capitals keeps it.** `* NASA plans` was read as the keyword `NASA`: search titled it "plans", and setting a TODO on it replaced "NASA". Only the note's own TODO keywords count as keywords now.
- `#+SEQ_TODO:` and `#+TYP_TODO:` lines are read like `#+TODO:` everywhere, and a line without `|` makes only its last word done, as in Org.
- The drawer's actions are labelled buttons: *New note*, *Today's daily*, *Capture an event*.

### Settings

- **Settings shows when the notes last synced,** under the server.
