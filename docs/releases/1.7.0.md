A tour to learn KorgNote by tapping: a note, *Welcome to KorgNote*, whose headings each show one of the app's gestures. It is offered at the end of the first start, and Settings → Tour opens it at any time, so if you are updating you can take it from there. The server is the same as 1.6.5's; updating it brings the tour to the web client too.

### Tour

- **A note to learn by tapping.** Most of what KorgNote does is a tap or a hold with no label to say so. The tour is an ordinary note, and each heading asks you to do one thing and says what it does: tap TODO to mark it done, tap a heading to edit it, hold one for its menu, tick checkboxes and watch the count in the heading follow, tap a date to find the heading in the calendar, open a table and recalculate its total, fold a heading, follow a link and come back. It ends with a few words on the drawer, search, the calendar, sharing to KorgNote and Settings.
- **Offered, never written unasked.** The welcome screens end with *Add the tour note* or *Skip*, since the note goes in with your own notes. Delete it when you are done.
- **Settings → Tour** opens it again. If the note is already there, half ticked through or added by another device, it is opened as it is rather than written over.
- In English and in French, as the app is.
- The date in it is today's, with no time, so it shows in the calendar but no reminder goes off.

### Settings

- **The last sync reads on its own line.** *Last synced 5 min ago* used to follow the error in one sentence, as in *Cannot reach the server… Last synced 5 min ago*. The time now stands alone, and why the last try failed is under it, smaller, since being offline is expected and the app works without the server.

### Fixes

- **A table cell's suggestions include the note's headings,** for a link, even when the cell is opened right after the note. They could be missing for as long as the cell's dialog was open.

### Documentation

- **What KorgNote supports of Org mode, feature by feature,** is now written down in [docs/org-features.md](https://github.com/jglouis/korgnote/blob/master/docs/org-features.md), linked from the README: for each part of the Org manual, what works, what works partly, what is only shown as written, and what is missing.
