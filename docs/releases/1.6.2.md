A fix for the server, which stopped seeing edits made on the computer after a folder in the notes was deleted or renamed. Also: the app opens where you left it, and the table editor completes links and dates. Update the server: the fix is in it.

### Sync

- **Edits made on the computer reach the phones again after a folder is deleted or renamed.** Deleting or renaming any folder in the notes directory silently stopped the server from noticing edits made outside it, until it restarted. Every phone still reported a good sync, and brought nothing new. The server now keeps watching through any change of folders, notices notes in a folder made or moved in, and checks the whole folder every minute for anything it was not told about.
- **The Devices page says whether the notes folder is watched.** Under *Notes folder* it shows the notes and folders watched and when they were last checked, and warns when watching fails or an edit was found that no file event reported. The details are in the server's log: `docker logs korgnote`. The log is also quieter: it no longer prints a stack trace each time a phone disconnects.

### Notes

- **The app opens where you left it:** on the same tab, the note you were reading, at the place you had read to. A note opened again from the drawer or a pin goes back to where you left it, rather than its top; a link, search or the agenda still go where they point. The calendar opens on the view you left it in.
- **The table editor's cells are highlighted and completed** as a line is in the element editor: links after `[[`, dates after `<` or `[`, and *Link* and *Date* buttons when there is nothing to suggest.
- **Holding a TODO keyword opens the heading's menu,** as holding the rest of the heading does, rather than changing the keyword when you lift your finger.

### Capture

- **A place shared from Google Maps gets its address.** Maps shares only a place's name and a short link; KorgNote now follows the link to find the address and pin, and writes `:LOCATION:` as the name, then the address. Offline, the place is its name and link, as before.

### Fixes

- On Android, the table editor's *Row*, *Column* and *Formulas* buttons stay above the keyboard while a cell is typed in, rather than hidden under it.
