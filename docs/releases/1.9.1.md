Three small things after 1.9.0: pairing says plainly where to press Allow, the event picker finds an event up to two years ahead, and the calendar uses the room a tablet or a browser window gives it. If you use the web app, update the server too, as it is served by it.

### Pairing

- **A dialog says where to press Allow.** Since 1.9.0 a new device waits for you to press Allow on the computer, but the phone said so in one small line at the bottom of Settings, easy to miss, and leaving the screen ended the wait without a word. A dialog now names the computer, says to press Allow next to this device's name on its Devices page, within two minutes, and stays until the computer answers or you press Cancel.

### Capture

- **The event picker's search reads two years ahead.** The picker lists the past week and the next two months, and its search only filtered that list: an event planned for next year could not be captured at all. A search now looks through the next two years, and the list before you search is as short as it was. A day in another year shows its year, and a search that finds nothing says how far it looked.

### Calendar

- **The day, the week and the month are drawn larger where there is room,** as on a tablet or in a browser window, where they were small print at a phone's sizes. A phone keeps its sizes, on its side too.

### Server

- Nothing changes in what the server does. The library that watches the notes folder is updated, and the image carries the web app with the larger calendar.
