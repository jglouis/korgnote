KorgNote speaks French, and gets ready for F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid.

### French

- **The whole app in French** when your phone is in French: menus, settings, the calendar, reminders and the widgets. Months and weekdays are named in your language too.
- On Android 13 and later, you can choose the app's language on its own, in the phone's Settings, under Apps, KorgNote, Language.

### Settings

- **Settings is now in sections,** each saying what it is for, so what you are looking for is easier to find.

### Widgets

- **The month widget's arrows** show the next or previous month at once, instead of after a delay when the app has not been opened for a while.
- **The agenda widget** shows each TODO keyword in its colour, as the app's agenda does.
- Both widgets show a preview in the launcher's list of widgets.

### Coming to F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid

- The APK no longer carries Google's encrypted list of the libraries it uses, which free app stores refuse.
- The app's description, icon and screenshots are in the source code, where those stores read them.
