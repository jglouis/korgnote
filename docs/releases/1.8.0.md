The calendar can be narrowed to the TODO keywords and tags you choose, and the agenda has a list of everything still to do, dated or not, as org-agenda's `t` gives. Both were asked for by people using the app. The server is the same as 1.7.0's; updating it brings these to the web client too.

### Before you update

- **The first start reads your notes once,** as it did after 1.6.5, since the app now keeps each note's open TODOs with its summary. Nothing is lost or changed. With a few megabytes of notes it takes a few seconds, and you can read and search your notes meanwhile.

### Filter

- **Tap a keyword or a tag in the agenda to see only what has it.** A second tap takes it away. A chip under the header says what the calendar is narrowed to, and its × removes it.
- **The filter applies to every view:** Day, Week, Month, the agenda and the TODO list. Clocked time is left whole, being what was done. Widgets and reminders ignore the filter.
- **A filter icon in the calendar's header** opens the whole choice: *Show done*, then every keyword and every tag in your notes. Among keywords, or among tags, one is enough; between the two, both are needed: `TODO` or `NEXT`, and `work`. *Clear* puts everything back.
- **The icon carries a dot** whenever something is left out or done entries show, so a calendar that looks empty says why. It replaces the ⋮ menu, which held only *Show done*.
- The filter lasts until the app is closed.

### TODO list

- **Every heading still to do, with or without a date.** A task with no date is on no day, so the calendar could not show it. The agenda now starts with a *TODOs · 8* row, which opens them on a page of their own.
- **Grouped by keyword,** in the order your notes declare them, each group most important first, then soonest due. A row says when it is due, in red once the date has passed.
- **A tap opens the heading,** and a hold offers the clock and the keywords, as in the agenda. A tap on a group's keyword or on a tag filters.
- **Search takes `todo:open`,** for headings whose keyword is any still to do, with words or other terms: `todo:open tag:work`.

### Calendar

- **The agenda opens on today.** It is the days from today on, so its tab and a start of the app now show today however far it was paged. Going back from a note opened from the agenda is the exception, and finds it where you left it. Week, Month and Day keep their place for the day, as before.
- **Fixed: the calendar could open on yesterday's week or month,** when the app had stayed open overnight. It now opens on today.
- **The calendar's floating button shows a calendar with a plus,** as it adds an event. The plain plus is the File tab's, which adds a heading.
