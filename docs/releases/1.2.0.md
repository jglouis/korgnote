The server is now optional: keep your notes in a folder on your phone instead. The first start asks which, and syncing with the server is faster and safer.

### Notes in a folder

- **Choose on the first start:** the app asks where your notes are, a folder on this device or your computer with the KorgNote server, and says what each costs. Change it later in Settings, under Notes.
- **Any folder another app syncs:** Syncthing, or Dropbox and others through an app like FolderSync. Or no sync at all. KorgNote writes your notes there and nothing else.
- **Edits made elsewhere** show within about ten seconds while the app is open, and when you come back to it. If a note changed in the folder while you were editing it, your version is kept beside it as a conflict copy, to compare and settle as with the server.
- On iOS, built from source, the folder can be on the iPhone, in iCloud Drive, or in Dropbox and other apps that show in Files.

### Sync with the server

- **First sync:** a newly paired phone gets all your notes in seconds instead of more than a minute.
- **Edits made at the same time:** a heading you are adding is no longer lost, or impossible to save, when the note changes on the computer meanwhile. Both are kept.
- A change is no longer sent to the server several times, which could happen when settling a conflict.

### Everything else

- The save button under an element being edited is no longer hidden behind the navigation buttons at the bottom of the screen.
- After a sync, the screen no longer freezes while the notes are read again, and only the notes that changed are read.
- Notifications are asked for once, on the first start. If you chose not now, turn them on in Settings, under Reminders.
- A pairing link opened while your notes are in a folder goes to pairing, instead of nowhere.
