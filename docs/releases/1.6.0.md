Clocking: track the time you spend on a heading, as Emacs does, and see where it went in the calendar. Also priorities, state changes logged with their notes, refiling, and conflicts settled change by change. Update the server too: the web client and the pairing page come with it.

### Clocking

- **Clock in on a heading** from its long-press menu, and out again, or cancel. KorgNote writes `CLOCK:` lines into the heading's `:LOGBOOK:` exactly as Emacs does, so either side sums what the other clocked. Clocking in stops any other clock running, in this note or another.
- **A bar on every tab shows the running clock:** the heading, how long it has run, and a stop button. Its switch button takes you back to a task you clocked recently, the way back after an interruption. For an hour after you stop, it offers *Clock back in*.
- **On Android, an ongoing notification** shows the clock with a timer, and its *Clock out* works with the app closed.
- **A clock started in Emacs, or on another device,** shows on the phone once it has synced, and the other way round.
- **Each heading shows the time spent on it** and under it, as `1 h 15`, against its effort when it has one: `1 h 15 / 2 h`, with a warning sign once it is past. *Set effort…* in the long-press menu sets it.
- **The calendar shows what the hours went on.** The day and week draw clocked time beside what was planned, with the total above them; the agenda ends with a report of the time clocked in its days, by note and heading.
- A long-press on a calendar entry clocks its heading in or out, and changes its TODO keyword.
- A clock left running for eight hours turns the bar to a warning, with *Clock out at…* for the time you really stopped.
- A clock edited by hand has its `=> 1:15` summed again when you save.

### Tasks

- **Priorities:** tap a heading's `[#A]`, or *Priority…* in its long-press menu, to pick another from the note's range (`#+PRIORITIES:`, numbers up to 64 too). The agenda and the calendar put the most important first within a time.
- **State changes are logged as each keyword asks:** `DONE(d!)` stamps the time, `WAIT(w@)` asks for a note, written under the state line as Org writes it, and into the `:LOGBOOK:` with `#+STARTUP: logdrawer`.
- **Refile a heading** under another heading or note, with its body and sub-headings, from *Refile…* in its long-press menu: Emacs's `C-c C-w`.
- **Every date opens its day in the calendar,** inactive ones included, and the long-press menu makes a date active or inactive.

### Sync

- **Settle a conflict change by change.** *Compare* now shows each change between the note and its conflict copy, under its heading: keep the note's lines, the copy's, both, or write them yourself, then apply. The two versions are told apart in red and green.

### Org

- **Worth knowing: a note without a `#+TODO:` line now reads only `TODO` and `DONE`,** as Org does. Before, it borrowed every keyword declared in any note. A note that relied on that needs a `#+TODO:` line of its own.
- Habits (`.+2d/4d`) repeat again; lists run on over one blank line and keep their numbering; an item keeps its paragraphs, blocks and tables; checkbox items in a numbered list keep their number.
- The agenda leaves out `COMMENT` headings and what is tagged `:ARCHIVE:`, as Org's does.
- `#+LINK:` abbreviations and `doi:` links open; search's `todo:NEXT` and `priority:A`, and group tags from `#+TAGS:`, find what they name; entities such as `\alpha`, `H_{2}O` and `<<targets>>` read as they print.

### Pairing

- **A failed pairing says why, and what to do,** in a dialog: not a pairing code, a code without an address, a used or expired code, or nothing listening on the computer.
- The server no longer makes a pairing code without an address a phone can reach: its Devices page says to type the computer's address, or to start the container with `KORGNOTE_ADVERTISED_ADDRESSES`.

### Fixes

- A heading's priority shows in the note again; it was saved but never drawn.
- Opening another note while an element is being edited no longer carries the edit across: an untouched one, such as a new note's empty heading, is dropped, and one with changes asks to be saved or discarded first.
- A new note opens at once, without waiting for the server.
- Picking a keyword or a priority no longer holds the screen while the agenda is rebuilt.
- Reminders show the KorgNote unicorn in the status bar instead of a calendar.
