The calendar has more room on a phone, and the server now comes as a Docker image.

### Calendar

- **Week:** the days scroll sideways, about three and a half on screen, so titles can be read. The
  week opens on today. Swipe again at Monday or Sunday to go to the week before or after.
- **Zoom the hours:** pinch the day or week view to make the hours taller or shorter, from the whole
  day on one screen to room for a 15-minute meeting. The zoom stays when you change day or week,
  and between the two. On the desktop, use Ctrl and the mouse wheel.
- **Month:** a busy week gets the height it needs, so titles get two or three lines instead of being
  cut short. A full month scrolls. The month no longer shows a last row with only the next month's
  days.
- **Day:** a long list of tasks without a time scrolls, instead of pushing the hours off the screen.
- Hours are taller by default, so a half-hour meeting shows its title. A time that does not fit in
  a short meeting is left out, instead of being cut in half.

### Server

- The server is published as a Docker image for amd64 and arm64, so you can run it without the
  source code. See "Docker" in the README.
