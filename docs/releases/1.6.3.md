The calendar can now move what is in it: hold an entry to reschedule it or change its deadline. Also: tap the period at the top to go straight to any date, and folds open and close the same way everywhere. Only the app changes; the server is the same as 1.6.2's.

### Calendar

- **Reschedule from the calendar.** Holding an entry offers *Reschedule…*, *Change deadline…* or *Change date…*, depending on what the date is, and opens the same date and time picker as the editor. A repeater or a warning period stays as written, and an event over several days keeps its length. If the note changed since the calendar showed it, its date is left alone and the app says so.
- **Go to any date.** A tap on the period at the top of the calendar opens a date picker, with a year list, so a day months or years away is one pick rather than many swipes. The calendar stays in the same view.
- **Showing done entries is a switch** in the new ⋮ menu at the end of the calendar's header, where the eye icon used to be. The eye didn't say what it hid, or whether it was hiding it.
- **"Capture an event" is now the calendar's + button,** rather than a button in the drawer of notes.

### Notes

- **Folds open and close the same way everywhere.** A chevron points right while closed and turns down while open, for the drawer's folders, a note's headings and the drawers in a note alike. In the drawer, folders used to point down while closed and up while open. A heading's contents, and a drawer's lines, now slide open and shut rather than appearing at once.
