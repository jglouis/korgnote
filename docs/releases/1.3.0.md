A third place for your notes, a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, as an experiment. Also: widgets for the Android home screen, saved searches, and your notes no longer in phone backups.

### Sync with a WebDAV server (experimental)

- **Nextcloud, a NAS or a hosting service** you already have can keep your notes instead of the KorgNote server, so no computer needs to stay on. Choose **A WebDAV server** on the first start, or in Settings under **Change where notes are**.
- **Give the folder's address, your user name and a password.** On Nextcloud, use an app password. The address must start with `https://`.
- **Checked before use:** the app makes sure the server refuses a write over a newer version of a note, so two devices cannot overwrite each other's edits. A server that does not is refused, and the app says why. So is one that asks to sign in another way than with a password, such as Digest.
- **Works like the KorgNote server:** the app keeps its own copy and works offline, edits made on two devices are merged, and a conflict keeps both versions. Changes made elsewhere show within about 30 seconds while the app is open.
- Tested with Nextcloud and Apache, not yet with NAS or hosting services. Please report what works and what does not.

### Widgets on the Android home screen

- **Agenda:** what is overdue, then the week from today. **Month:** a dot for each entry, with arrows to move between months.
- Tap an entry to open its note at its heading, or a day to open the calendar there.
- A **sync button** syncs from the home screen, even with the app closed. The widgets say when your notes last synced.

### Saved searches

- **Keep a search by name** with the bookmark in the search field, and run it again from the drawer on the Search tab. Long-press one to rename or remove it.
- Saved searches are kept on this device, like pins: nothing in your notes changes.

### Privacy

- **Your notes are no longer in phone backups,** nor moved to a new phone: on Android, neither in Google's backup nor in a transfer between phones; on iOS, built from source, neither in iCloud nor in computer backups. They stay where you keep them. On a new phone, choose again where your notes are, and they sync.
- **The pairing with your computer, and the WebDAV password, are encrypted** with a key that never leaves the phone: the Android Keystore, or the iPhone's Keychain. They no longer travel in backups either, so a new phone pairs again.
- Updating keeps your pairing: it is encrypted when the new version first starts.
